# Full test sets, four additional benchmarks (2026-10-28)

Paired comparison: same documents, same prompts, both decoders in one process on one GPU,
batch 1, greedy, bf16. `speedup` = decode tokens/s over full-depth decoding **in the same job**
(tokens and seconds summed over the task's shards). `p` = two-sided paired sign test over
documents against full depth, counting only documents where the two disagree. `sig` = p < .05.

Per-document scores stay out of git:
`eval/results/SCORE_<task>.json`.
