# Entry-signal probe: why does a token entering at the last pass fail without training?

_written 2026-10-28 22:03:59 by entry_analyze.py; raw = probes/entry/results/<model>/raw_<task>.pkl; code = MARS/integration/probes/entry_probe/_

## Answer

**Mostly a coordinate gap, plus a smaller information gap that a better entry vector cannot close.** Look at the two models where late entry fails without training (sft and stock26, GSM8K, maps fit on GSM8K). A token-only entry in the right coordinates closes 70-78 % of the agreement gap between E and the oracle (LE; the token-mean lookup TM closes 67-77 %), and 86-88 % of the NLL gap. "Token-only" means the same token identity, no context, model frozen, mapped by a linear map (LE) or a per-token lookup (TM). Agreement moves from .706 / .751 to .930 / .919 (oracle .995 / .992). Adding the predecessor's deep state (LEH) brings this to 80-82 %, i.e. .939 / .949. The last ~20 % of the agreement gap (~10 % of the NLL gap) needs the position's own three passes of context processing: only ORA recovers it. On IFEval (maps fit on IFEval) the split is closer to even. LE closes 46-51 % of the agreement gap (TM 41-45 %) and ~70 % of the NLL gap; LEH closes 68-72 % and 87-89 %.

What the "coordinate" fix is, and what it is not:

- **Not scale or normalisation.** The raw embedding is tiny (median norm 0.7-1.0, against 37-51 for a real pass-4 input). But rescaling self.norm(E) to the typical norm (NE) makes things much worse: agreement .25 / .22 on GSM8K, against .71 / .75 for E. The direction is wrong, not only the length. The right direction needs a learned map from the token to its typical deep state.
- **Not the tied output embedding.** This was verified in the modeling code. The loop input is self.norm(h), and lm_head reads exactly that state, so W_out rows do live in the loop-input space. They encode "the next token is v", not "this token is v". So OE, OSE and OE+H, like H itself, make the slot **copy its own token**: argmax = x_q at 92-100 % of positions, and agreement drops to .01-.04. In this space the state that predicts x_q is the predecessor's state.
  - The information is all there, one position shifted. A linear map of W_out[x] (LOE) is exactly as good as one of E[x] (LE): .929 vs .930 for sft on GSM8K.
  - The two matrices are nearly unrelated: centered cosine of W_out[v] with E[v] is 0.13 over the vocabulary.
- **A learnable shared map, not a free one.** A map fit on one domain transfers poorly. GSM8K maps close only 6 % (sft) and 22 % (stock26) of the IFEval gap. IFEval maps close 48-60 % of the GSM8K gap, against 70-78 % in domain. Only 46-61 % of IFEval tokens occur in the GSM8K fit set, and even tokens that do occur transfer worse.
  - But a single map fit on both domains pooled is within 1 point of each in-domain map on both tasks: sft .929 / .715 vs .930 / .719; stock26 .913 / .820 vs .919 / .828.
  - So one linear coordinate change works for both kinds of text. It only needs to have seen that kind of text.
  - The lower token-only share on IFEval (45-51 % of the gap) is therefore not a fitting artefact. IFEval text needs more context from the position itself.

Soft embeddings (SE family) do not help.

- On the model's own text the predecessor's argmax is x_q at 98.7-99.6 % of positions, with mean p(x_q) of .89-.93 on GSM8K and .75-.89 on IFEval.
- So SE ≈ E at the 50-81 % of positions where the predecessor is confident and correct (cos(SE, E) = 1.000 there).
- Where SE differs from E, it is worse than E. At positions where the predecessor's argmax is wrong, agreement is .28-.40 for SE against .45-.94 for E.
- The SE variants never beat their E counterparts by more than noise. LSE is below LE in every model, task and fit. SE and NSE are at most 0.3 points above E and NE.

The trained model (v2) shows what training buys. Its raw E reaches .962 on GSM8K and .887 on IFEval at pass 4, better than any frozen-model entry including its own LE and LEH (GSM8K .941 / .947, IFEval .763 / .835 with IFEval maps). Training taught the pass-4 layers to read the embedding coordinates, and did so better than a linear map. What remains to the oracle is 3.4 points on GSM8K and 9.6 on IFEval: the information part.

Checks:

- ORA reproduces the full forward's pass 4. Logits agree to ≤1.1e-4 and the state to ≤5.3e-5 (fp32 on GPU), and agree4 = 1.000.
- On CPU (tiny Ouro, fp32), ORA substituted into the gate mixture reproduces HF's full-depth logits to 1.1e-6.
- E is identical to mech_core's cell(4,1): 0 max diff on CPU and GPU. On v2's text its argmax matches the 10-23 mech run on 100 % of positions (GSM8K and IFEval).

## Setup

Ouro-2.6B, T = 4 passes of one 48-layer stack; teacher forcing on prompt + the model's OWN full-depth (ar:4) generation (campaign shards, campaign chat template). For every generated position q (token x_q, successor x_{q+1} also generated) a 1-pass LATE slot runs at pass index 4 and reads the IDEAL deep context: the true pass-4 keys/values of all positions < q from the normal full-depth forward, plus its own key. Only its entry state (its input to pass 4) changes between rows below. Readout = its pass-4 logits (the gate mixture renormalised to the one pass it ran: weight 1). fp32, TF32 off. Linear maps (ridge, standardised features, intercept unpenalised, lambda chosen on held-out docs 550-599, refit on 300-599) are fit on GSM8K docs 300-599 to predict the TRUE pass-4 input of the same position; the model is frozen. c = median norm of real pass-4 inputs on the fit set. Verified in modeling_ouro.py: each pass ends with `hidden = self.norm(hidden)`; that normed state is the next pass's input AND what lm_head / the exit gate read (no further norm); tie_word_embeddings = False, so lm_head rows W_out[v] live in the loop-input space while embedding rows E[v] do not.

Metrics: **agree** = argmax equals the full-depth (gate-mixture) prediction at q; **agree4** = equals the full forward's pass-4 readout (ORA = 1 by construction); **NLL** of x_{q+1} (nats); **cos_out** = cosine of the slot's normed pass-4 output with the true pass-4 output state; **cos_in** = cosine of the entry with the true pass-4 input. 95 % CIs: bootstrap over documents (B = 2000). **closed** = share of the E -> ORA agreement gap closed. Entries marked * are controls added beyond the brief.
